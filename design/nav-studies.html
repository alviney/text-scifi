The map

  • The bar is the season. Marker size is the estimated haul, so the big one at d41 reads before any number does. Hollow means never surveyed — unknown rather than merely uncertain.
  • Worked rocks stay on the rail behind you, filled and grey. The season visibly accumulates rather than resetting.
  • Five legs are two pixels, along the bottom edge of the leg strip.
  • Three parallax layers. Speed and brightness both fall off with distance, and the ship holds station mid-strip, so the drift reads as the leg passing rather than the ship flying.

Stage goals

Targets, not a score. §1 says the outcome spectrum is narrative and has no points in it, so these are what the departure crew expect of this stage — goals you can fall short of and still arrive, just with less.

  • Every goal reads x/y, including the header. One shape for "how far through" whether the unit is beds, rations, rocks or condition — so the column scans as a column instead of five sentences.
  • Two scales, again. Five stage goals you can still act on, and one standing line for the things that run the whole voyage. The voyage line has no bars because nothing you do this week moves it much.
  • The red stripe is the hierarchy. Two objectives end the run if you ignore them — food and the locker. The other three cost you an outcome, not the colony. That is what makes "critical" mean something rather than decorating every row.
  • State is glyph, colour and bar together — ● ◐ ○ never carry it on colour alone.
  • Every target is a quantity the simulation already has. Beds built, rations in the locker, rare compounds banked, objects worked, reactor condition. Nothing here is a new mechanic — it is the existing state, told as expectations.
  • Deadline is implied, not printed. Every stage goal means "before you go dark", so saying it on each row would be five copies of one sentence.

Dropped, and easy to put back

Three ahead

The phase strip said day 34 · 3 ahead. The rail already shows three hollow diamonds to the right of the ship, so the count was the picture written out in words.

The clock row

Y2 day 34 09:00 is gone entirely. The day is in the phase strip and the hour is not a decision — it earns its place on a screen with a running job on it, not here.

"Leg 1 of 5"

Gone from the strip. The five segments underneath say the same thing in a form you do not have to read, and the leg's name is the part worth the width.

The voyage caption

y2 belt / y283 debris went with it. Three hundred years is context, not a number anyone acts on from this screen.

The goal sub-lines

"Nothing grows in the dark. The next crew eat what you plant." and the other two went with the rename. A goal that needs a sentence under it is a goal whose label is wrong — and the sentences pushed the fifth row off a short screen.

38% and the object list

Everything below the map went. The percentage was the rail read back as a number, and the list was the rail read back as words — the same thing three times. What is under the map should be what the map cannot say.
