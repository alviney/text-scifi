The detail window

Tapping a goal is the one place on this screen with room for a voice. plan.md §5c already asks whether inherited things should carry authorship — "set by Marchetti, y0" — and calls it cheap, and the thing that makes them feel like a legacy rather than a default. A goal is exactly that: somebody decided it, before you existed, and wrote down why. Three ways to spend that.

Where I'd land

P2 and D1. The bar is the only thing on the overview saying what x/y already said, and dropping it hands the weight back to the map — which is the thing this screen is named after. D1 because it is the only version that could not be generated from the numbers: a person, a sentence, and a reason, written before the ship left.

D2's log is the one worth stealing from. "Bed 1 planted — first crop d55" against a name is a better record of a season than any summary, and it is all state the simulation already emits to the feed. It could sit under D1's quote rather than replacing it.

D3 is right about one thing the others miss: the consequence should be arithmetic, not adjectives. "Empty on day 51" is worth more than "the next crew go hungry", and it is a number the simulation can actually produce.
