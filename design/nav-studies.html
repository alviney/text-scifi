What's on it

  • The bar is the season. 38% is not a mood, it is 56 days and three rocks.
  • Marker size is the estimated haul. The big one at d41 reads before any number does — that is the whole reason objects are on screen rather than only in the list.
  • Hollow means never surveyed. Unknown rather than merely uncertain, which is the distinction the survey mechanic turns on.
  • Worked rocks stay on the rail behind you, filled and grey. The season visibly accumulates rather than resetting.
  • Five legs are two pixels. The voyage indicator is the bottom edge of the leg strip rather than a row of its own — present, countable, and never competing with the season it sits above.
  • Three parallax layers at different speeds and brightnesses. Distant stars barely move; the near ones cross in seconds. The ship holds station in the middle of the strip and the season moves past it, so the drift reads as the leg passing rather than as the ship flying.

Three ahead

The phase strip said day 34 · 3 ahead. The rail already shows three hollow diamonds to the right of the ship, so the count was the picture written out in words.

The clock row

Y2 day 34 09:00 is gone entirely. The day is in the phase strip and the hour is not a decision — it earns its place on a screen with a running job on it, not here.

"Leg 1 of 5"

Gone from the strip. The five segments underneath say the same thing in a form you do not have to read, and the leg's name is the part worth the width.
